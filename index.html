Ingredients & Story

Sixty-five years of Vietnamese skincare

Thorakao is a Vietnamese skincare name that many families have trusted for generations. It grew from homemade cream made with local ingredients into a brand known for its turmeric cream, ginseng cream and pomelo flower shampoo.

Timeline

  1. 1957 Mrs. Lan Hảo starts making skin cream from local ingredients.
  2. 1961 The Lan Hảo company is founded and the Thorakao name is born.
  3. 1969 Thorakao runs six branches in southern Vietnam and one in Cambodia.
  4. 1987 The company returns after closing in 1975.
  5. Today Creams, cleansers and hair care found across Vietnam.
